## Problem

Manual interview coordination between recruiters and candidates caused scheduling conflicts and lost feedback.

## Solution

Built 15+ REST APIs in Spring Boot for scheduling, candidate management, and feedback workflows, secured with JWT-based role access.

## Outcome

Cut manual coordination effort by 40% and improved backend response times by 30% for 100+ active users.

## Learnings

Gained production experience in JWT auth, role-based access control, and query optimization under real load.
