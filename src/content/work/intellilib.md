## Problem

Libraries needed a centralized way to manage their catalog and book issuance, and readers needed help finding relevant books.

## Solution

Built a centralized library platform with Spring Boot and MongoDB that automates catalog management and book issuance, secured with JWT, with an ANN-based recommendation engine at its core.

## Outcome

The recommendation engine reached 88.6% accuracy with a P@10 of 0.77. The research was presented at the International Symposium on AI Frontiers 2.0, where it won the Best Paper award.
